The equation behind it

When the planet's path is free of spots, the measured depth is the true depth times a correction ε that depends on wavelength:

ε(λ) = 1 / [ 1 − f_spot · (1 − S_spot/S_phot) − f_fac · (1 − S_fac/S_phot) ]

f is the share of the star's light that the spots or faculae take up. S is the brightness of each surface type at that wavelength. Put a spot in the planet's path and this simple formula no longer holds: the planet blocks the dim spot itself, and the dip gets shallower instead.

Illustrative model: simplified stellar spectra (blackbodies), round-number temperatures, and a made-up planet. The Pandora bands are the mission's published ones (visible 0.4 to 0.7 µm, near-infrared 0.9 to 1.6 µm).
